Building a Personal Website Using AI

What happened when I treated AI as a collaborator—not a magic button—and rebuilt my corner of the internet one decision at a time.

September 22, 20268 min read

I had wanted a personal website for years. Not a portfolio assembled from a template, but a place that felt like mine: a record of projects, questions, and things I was still figuring out.

The obstacle was never the idea. It was translating that idea into a system I could actually maintain.

AI changed the starting point

Instead of beginning with a blank editor, I began with a conversation. I described the experience I wanted, inspected the result, and made one decision at a time.

The useful shift was not “AI can build my website.” It was “AI can help me reason through each layer of the website.”

# BUILDING IN PUBLIC

Building a Personal Website Using AI

published: 2026-09-22reading_time: 8 minstatus: shipped

I had wanted a personal website for years. The hard part was not writing code—it was deciding what the site should say, how it should feel, and what I could realistically maintain.

01. Start with decisions, not components

I used AI as a thinking partner. Each iteration answered one concrete question:

  • What must a visitor understand in the first ten seconds?
  • Which content deserves its own route?
  • What should remain deliberately simple?

Observation: AI made iteration cheaper. It did not make product judgment optional.
